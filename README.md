## Systematic and Quantitative Engines

### Portfolio Optimization Engine
Implements mean variance, Black Litterman, and hierarchical risk parity.  
Includes covariance cleaning, rolling backtests, and constrained portfolio construction.

### Factor Backtest Engine
Multi factor model across more than 500 equities over ten years.  
Cross sectional ranking, quintile portfolios, and full performance evaluation.  
Quality factor Sharpe ratio of 1.55.

### TSMOM Engine
Cross asset time series momentum strategy based on Moskowitz 2012.  
Long short implementation across 13 ETFs with signal driven allocation.

### Volatility Regime Engine
Regime detection using hidden Markov models and rule based overlays.  
Volatility targeting applied to a multi asset portfolio.
